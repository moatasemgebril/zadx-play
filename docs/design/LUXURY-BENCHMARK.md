# Zad X Play: Luxury and Premium Design Benchmark (2026-11-23)

Author: **zadx-kids-art-director**. Scope: how premium the child-facing look and feel is, compared with the
five kids' apps best known for polished design. Inputs: code review of `lib/core/theme/`,
`lib/shared/widgets/zad_widgets.dart`, `lib/shared/art/`, `lib/features/{home,games,shell,tasks,edu_games}`,
`docs/design/ART-AUDIT.md`, plus the public store pages and award pages cited below.
No code was changed. Nothing here copies another app's characters, logos or art.

Islamic context (not in the top five for polish, but the closest audience):

## 2. Scored comparison (1–10)

How the scores were set: Zad X scores come from the code review (not yet seen on a real device). Benchmark scores
are the Art Director's estimate from public store pages, screenshots and the awards above. They are not from
hands-on testing in this cycle. Cultural/Islamic fit is scored for a Muslim family audience (modest characters,
no music by default, respectful Quran treatment). It is not a judgement of the other apps' quality for their own audience.

Best in class: **Pok Pok, 91**. Zad X is **33 points behind**. Almost all of the gap is in character (−6 to −8),
motion (−5), reward moments (−6), onboarding (−6) and lighting depth (−4). Zad X leads on Islamic fit and Arabic typography.

### Why Zad X scored what it did (from the code)

- **Hierarchy 6**: one title style per screen, but the hero cards (`home_screen.dart` `_Hero`, `games_screen.dart` `_GamesHero`) are flat
  colour boxes, so they carry no stronger visual weight than the content cards below them.
- **Lighting 5**: the area scenes (`lib/shared/art/scenes`) give a good base. Cards (`ZadCard`) have one flat white fill and one
  shadow (`0x1A6C3BEB`, blur 18), so there is no key light, no highlight and no material feel. Hero and stat tiles are flat fills.
- **Mascot 2**: there is no brand character. The only figures are the faceless exercise kids (good, but they are utility art).
- **Motion 5**: the scenes loop slowly and the nav icons pop. But page transitions are the platform default (no
  `pageTransitionsTheme` or `CustomTransitionPage`), buttons use Material ink only, there is no spring physics and no staggered entrance.
- **Reward 3**: `praise_dialog.dart` is a stock `AlertDialog` with a Material `Icons.star_rounded`. `ZadCelebrationBurst` (1.2 s) is
  nice but small. Nothing builds anticipation and nothing pays it off.
- **Sound 5**: there are 8 short SFX and no music, which is the correct direction. They have not been checked for one tonal family or for loudness.
- **Typography 7**: Tajawal, Baloo Bhaijaan 2 and Amiri Quran ship in the bundle (offline, OFL), and the Quran line height is 2.1.
  Missing: a type scale with named roles, tuned Arabic line heights for Tajawal body text (~1.5), and tabular figures for times and minutes.
- **Onboarding 2**: there is no first-run welcome flow for the child (no "onboard" anything in `lib/`).
- **Parent 6**: the light lattice and no motion are right. The cards are still generic Material.
- **Accessibility 7**: contrast is unit-tested, reduce-motion is respected and RTL is mirrored. It loses points because nothing has been checked on a device yet and some tap targets on the game tiles are tight.
- **Islamic 10**: no figures of prophets, angels or companions; no music; the Quran night scene is calm; the exercise kids wear modest clothes.

Stock pieces that still break the premium feel (concrete list):
`Icons.nightlight_round` / `Icons.wb_sunny_rounded` in the Home hero; `Icon(game.icon)` in `_ZadGameTile`;
`Icons.star_rounded` in the praise dialog; the default `AlertDialog` shape; default route transitions.

## 3. Five ranked luxury upgrades

All five follow the same rules: only original art authored by us, Islamic review first, reduce-motion gives a still frame,
no flashing (< 3 per second), 60 fps on low-end Android, and never casino-like (no loot boxes, no random rewards,
no slot/jackpot reels, no coin showers, no countdown pressure, no red "claim now" urgency).

### U1. Soft depth and lighting system ("Zad Surface"). Effort: M. Owner: zadx-frontend-dev

What: one shared material for every card, tile and hero, so the whole app reads as one premium family.

- `ZadSurface` replaces the body of `ZadCard`. It has:
  - two shadows: an ambient one (blur 24, y 8, purple at 8 %) and a key one (blur 6, y 2, ink at 6 %);
  - a 1 px inner top highlight (white at 60 %, drawn with a `foregroundDecoration` gradient);
  - a very soft vertical gradient fill (white → cream #FFF8F0 at 4 %);
  - large radii (24 for cards, 20 for tiles, 999 for chips) with continuous corners where it is cheap.
- Hero cards become **lit gradients** per area (Home: sunrise gold → sky; Games: purple → sky; Tasks: green → mint;
  Quran: navy → ink with a gold rim). The text must keep ≥ 4.5:1 contrast at the gradient's worst stop. Extend
  `test/kids_art_test.dart` to check it.
- **Subtle grain**: generate one 64×64 noise `ui.Image` at startup in code (no bitmap asset), and paint it with
  `ImageShader(TileMode.repeated)` at 2–3 % opacity on heroes and scenes only. It is drawn once and cached inside `RepaintBoundary`.
- **"Glass" without `BackdropFilter`**: the blur is too expensive on low-end Android. Fake it with a semi-opaque white
  fill (white at 72 %), a white border at 40 % and the highlight above. Allow a real blur only on the shell header, and only if the device check stays at 60 fps.
- Tokens go in `lib/core/theme/` (`ZadElevation.low/mid/high`, `ZadRadii`, `ZadGradients`), so no screen hard-codes a shadow again.

Child-safety / Islamic check: purely decorative and no new content. It keeps contrast tested. The Quran surfaces stay matte and calm (no sheen, no grain motion).

### U2. Gold-foil "Star Seal" reward reveal. Effort: M. Owners: zadx-frontend-dev + zadx-illustrator

What: replace `showPraiseDialog`'s stock `AlertDialog` with a branded full-card moment that feels earned.

- Sequence (≤ 1.6 s total): the card rises with a spring (`SpringSimulation`, damping ratio ~0.7). An original
  eight-point gold star seal (`CustomPainter`, layered gold #FFC83D → #E0A21A with a darker rim) scales in from 0.6.
  A single **foil sheen** sweeps across it once: a `LinearGradient` shader whose stops are translated by the animation, inside `RepaintBoundary`.
  Then the praise phrase fades up in `AppTheme.display`, and `ZadCelebrationBurst` plays once behind it.
- Haptic: one `HapticFeedback.mediumImpact()` when the seal lands. Sound: the existing `star.wav`, which stays off if the parent turned SFX off.
- Reduce motion: the seal is shown at full size with a still sheen highlight. No sweep and no burst.
- **Deterministic**: the same action always gives the same visible reward. No mystery box, no "spin", no random
  multipliers, no stacking coins. The reward is praise plus earned minutes the parent already approved.
- Reuse it for adhkar section done (ART-AUDIT open item 5), but with a quieter variant: a silver-gold crescent seal, no burst, no sound.

Child-safety / Islamic check: no gambling mechanics. Praise phrases are already reviewed per variant (`data/catalog/praise.dart`).
It is not used on Quran recitation itself (no "score" on the Quran).

### U3. Unified motion system (spring press, transitions, stagger). Effort: M. Owner: zadx-frontend-dev

- `ZadPressable` wraps every tappable card and button. On press it scales to 0.96, and on release a spring brings it back
  (`AnimationController.animateWith(SpringSimulation(...))`). It plays `HapticFeedback.selectionClick()` on child screens only.
- **Page transitions**: use go_router `CustomTransitionPage`, or a `pageTransitionsTheme` set in `AppTheme`. Use a
  shared-axis style: a 24 dp slide plus a fade, 280 ms, `Curves.easeOutCubic`, mirrored for RTL. Quran routes get a 400 ms pure fade.
- **Hero** from a game tile cover into its game screen header, which gives the "magic" continuity premium apps have.
- **Staggered entrance** of cards on the first build of a tab: 40 ms apart, 220 ms each, translate 12 dp plus fade. It plays once per session and never on a tab switch-back.
- Everything checks `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`. If it is set, there is no scale, no slide, only an instant fade-in or nothing.
- Reasons to use spring physics over long tweens: they feel tactile (the Pok Pok quality) and stay short.

Child-safety / Islamic check: no flashing and no fast loops. Motion is user-initiated or happens once. The Quran stays the calmest area.

### U4. Original illustrated covers and heroes (replace stock icons). Effort: M. Owner: zadx-illustrator

- Each game tile gets an original **cover scene** drawn in `CustomPainter`, 16:10, in the Games island family. Examples:
  Letters = floating Arabic letter blocks on an island; Mosque builder = dome and minaret pieces stacking up; Prophets'
  stories = a scenery-only landscape (desert, ark-shaped hill, palm trees), with **no figures** at all.
- The Home hero swaps its Material sun/moon for the small town and mosque from the Home scene, plus a gold crescent. The Tasks hero shows a mini garden.
- The praise and lock banners use `ZadIcon` instead of Material icons.
- Every cover is static by default. On the selected or focused tile, one element can drift slowly (lantern sway < 2°, a cloud drifting).
- Keep the tile button ≥ 48 dp tall and the title ≥ 15 sp. Move "Play" onto the cover as a pill, so the whole tile is one big target.

Child-safety / Islamic check: scenery, objects and architecture only. No music notes or instruments. The Prophets cover must be checked by the scholar review before it ships.

### U5. Original mascot plus first-run welcome. Effort: L. Owners: zadx-illustrator → scholar review → zadx-frontend-dev

- **Direction**: a small glowing **lantern companion** (فانوس) in brand gold and purple with an eight-point-star window.
  It shows emotion through **glow, tilt and bounce**, not through a face. That is the default, faceless and safe for every
  family. A version with simple dot eyes is optional and goes ahead **only if the scholar review approves it** (some families avoid faces on animate characters).
  It must not look like any existing brand's mascot. Run a similarity check against the benchmark casts before final art.
- Where it appears: Home greeting, onboarding, reward reveal (it holds the seal), empty states. On the Quran and Adhkar screens it is
  only a still, dim lantern in the scene, not a character.
- **First-run welcome** (3 screens, under 30 s): greeting → choose your lantern colour (no name or photo needed;
  a nickname stays optional and on the device) → "Ask a grown-up to finish setup", which goes to the parent gate. No account and no data collection in the child flow.
- Tech: start with `CustomPainter` plus springs (zero dependencies, fully ours). If we later need a rich state machine
  (idle, happy, sleepy), use **Rive**: the `rive` Flutter runtime is MIT-licensed, and we author the `.riv` file ourselves.
  **Lottie** (`lottie` package, MIT) is fine only for animations we author. Never use LottieFiles or Rive Marketplace community
  files unless the licence is CC0 and recorded in `docs/` (most are not cleared for commercial use). Keep each file < 150 KB and pause it off-screen.

Child-safety / Islamic check: modest, faceless by default, no music, no data capture in onboarding, and owner plus scholar sign-off before it ships.

## 4. Grade

**Current visual design (premium/luxury lens): C+**

The area scenes, icon set, Islamic treatment and accessibility testing are a solid **B** foundation. But the
surfaces, reward moments, motion and lack of character keep it well below the benchmark.

Gaps to reach **A**:

1. Every card, tile and hero uses one depth system (U1). No hard-coded flat boxes or single shadows remain.
2. Zero stock Material icons on child screens (U4, plus the praise dialog and hero icons).
3. A branded, deterministic reward reveal on task done, game level done and adhkar section done (U2).
4. One motion language: spring press, directional RTL-aware transitions, Hero into games, reduce-motion fallbacks (U3).
5. An original mascot direction approved by the owner and the scholar review, plus a first-run welcome (U5).
6. A type scale with named roles (display / title / body / caption / numerals), Arabic line height ~1.5 for Tajawal body text, and tabular figures for times.
7. SFX checked for one soft tonal family and a peak level of −14 LUFS or lower. No music anywhere by default.
8. A device check on a low-end Android at 60 fps with the performance overlay, and golden screenshots with a bundled test font.
9. The Quality Gate grades every area A on `reports/quality/scorecard.md`.

Expected after U1–U4: **B+ / A-**. After U5 and the device check: **A**.
