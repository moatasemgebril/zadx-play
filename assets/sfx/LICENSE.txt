Zad X Play - sound effects
==========================

All audio files in this folder (tap.wav, correct.wav, wrong.wav, star.wav,
level_up.wav, task_complete.wav, unlock.wav, transition.wav)
are ORIGINAL works, synthesised from scratch by tools/gen_sfx.py using only
the Python standard library (sine tones, envelopes and filtered noise).
No third-party samples, recordings or loops were used or downloaded.

To regenerate (output is deterministic):
    python3 tools/gen_sfx.py

Change record: 2026-11-20 - music_calm.wav (background music) removed from the
app and from tools/gen_sfx.py by owner decision. Zad X Play ships no music.
